# Входной тест
Вопросы идут от простых к сложным: 1–10 — A1, 11–20 — A2, 21–30 — B1. Выбери один вариант в каждом вопросе. Если не знаешь — не угадывай наугад, выбери то, что звучит правильнее всего.

1. She ___ a teacher. :: am | is* | are
2. They ___ from Italy. :: is | are* | am
3. I ___ coffee every morning. :: drink* | drinks | drinking
4. He ___ like fish. :: don't | doesn't* | isn't
5. ___ you speak French? :: Are | Do* | Does
6. There ___ two books on the table. :: is | are* | be
7. This is ___ umbrella. :: a | an* | the
8. My sister's name is Kate. ___ is 20. :: He | She* | It
9. I can ___ very well. :: swims | swim* | to swim
10. We ___ TV now. :: watch | watches | are watching*
11. Yesterday we ___ to the cinema. :: go | went* | goed
12. ___ you at home last night? :: Did | Was | Were*
13. She ___ breakfast when I called. :: is having | was having* | has had
14. I ___ visit my grandma tomorrow. :: am going to* | go to | going
15. This book is ___ than that one. :: more interesting* | interestinger | most interesting
16. You ___ wear a seat belt. It's the law. :: must* | can | might
17. I have never ___ sushi. :: ate | eaten* | eat
18. She has lived here ___ 2019. :: for | since* | from
19. How ___ water do you drink a day? :: many | much* | lot
20. He is the ___ student in our class. :: taller | most tallest | tallest*
21. If it rains tomorrow, we ___ at home. :: stay | will stay* | would stay
22. If I ___ you, I would take the job. :: am | were* | would be
23. The window ___ by a boy yesterday. :: broke | was broken* | has broke
24. She asked me where I ___. :: live | lived* | am living
25. I'm not used to ___ up early. :: get | getting* | got
26. By the time we arrived, the film ___. :: already started | had already started* | has already started
27. The man ___ lives next door is a doctor. :: who* | which | whose
28. I wish I ___ more free time. :: have | had* | would have
29. He suggested ___ to the cinema. :: to go | going* | go
30. You ___ have seen him — he left an hour ago. :: can't* | mustn't | needn't
